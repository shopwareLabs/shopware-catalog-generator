## Changes

- The GLB files are unmodified copies of the `glTF-Binary` variants.
- The videos were converted from VP9 WebM to H.264 MP4 (no audio) for Safari support:
  `ffmpeg -i in.webm -c:v libx264 -preset slow -crf 26 -pix_fmt yuv420p -movflags +faststart -an out.mp4`.
